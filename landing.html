An editorial note
§ 01 — Capability

Three tools.
One pipeline.

Designed to take you from raw footage to a publishable splat in a single workspace.

01 — Train

Train a scene from photographs or video.

Drop a short video or a folder of photos. The pipeline extracts frames with ffmpeg, recovers camera poses through COLMAP, and trains a splatfacto model with nerfstudio — fully on your local GPU.

~30 min · RTX 3090 · runs entirely offline
02 — Edit

Sculpt the gaussians.

Select and delete unwanted gaussians with box, lasso, brush, flood, or sphere tools. Adjust transforms, combine scenes, crop space.

03 — Publish

Export anywhere.

Save as .ply, .spz, or .ssproj. Generate LODs for web embedding, publish directly to the cloud, or hand off to any engine that speaks Gaussian Splat.

.ply · .spz · .ssproj · LOD · iframe
04 — Compatibility

Every splat format.

Open and inspect .ply, .splat, .sog, .ksplat, .spz, .ssproj. Convert between any pair. Preview every gaussian in a real-time WebGL viewport.

05 — Privacy

100% offline.

Your footage never leaves the machine. No accounts. No cloud upload.

06 — Performance

Real-time GPU monitoring.

Live VRAM telemetry, COLMAP progress per phase, splatfacto step counter streamed via SSE. You always know what's running and how long it'll take.

SSE · per-phase progress · live VRAM & ETA
§ 02 — Process

From capture
to render.

Four stages. One dispatch. Streamed back to your browser in real time.

i.

Upload

Drop a video (MP4 · MOV · MKV) or a folder of photos. The pipeline auto-detects the input type.

MP4 · MOV · MKV · JPG · PNG
ii.

COLMAP

Feature extraction and matching recover camera poses from overlapping images. Validates ≥ 20 registered cameras.

sequential · vocab_tree
iii.

Train

nerfstudio splatfacto trains up to 30 000 iterations on your local GPU. Progress streams in real time via SSE.

splatfacto · live SSE
iv.

Edit

The finished PLY loads directly into the editor. Crop, clean, transform, and publish without leaving the browser.

PLY · SPZ · SSPROJ
