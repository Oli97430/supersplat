Photographs hold more than memory — they encode geometry, light, and depth. OneClick SPLAT extracts all of it, turning a short video or folder of photos into a photorealistic 3D Gaussian Splat you can edit, crop, and publish — entirely in your browser, entirely offline.

What it does

Three tools.
One pipeline.

From raw footage to a polished, publishable 3D scene — every step runs in a single integrated workspace.

01
Starting from scratch

Train a scene

Drop a video or folder of photos. The pipeline extracts frames with ffmpeg, recovers camera poses through COLMAP, and trains a splatfacto model with nerfstudio — all on your local GPU, no cloud.

~30 min · RTX 3090 · runs fully offline
02
Refine & sculpt

Edit the splat

Select and delete unwanted Gaussians with box, lasso, brush, flood, or sphere tools. Adjust transforms, combine multiple splats, crop the scene, and inspect per-Gaussian data in real time.

.ply · .splat · .sog · .ksplat · .spz · .ssproj
03
Share your work

Publish

Export optimised .ply or compressed .spz files. Publish directly to the cloud, generate LODs for web embedding, or hand off to any engine that speaks Gaussian Splat.

cloud · download · iframe embed · LOD
The pipeline

From capture
to render.

01

Upload

Drop a video (MP4, MOV, MKV) or a folder of photos. Input type is auto-detected.

02

COLMAP

Feature extraction and matching recover camera poses from overlapping images. Sequential or vocab_tree.

03

Train

nerfstudio's splatfacto trains up to 30 000 iterations on your GPU. Progress streams in real time via SSE.

04

Load & Edit

The finished PLY loads directly into the editor. Crop, clean, transform, and publish — never leaving the browser.

~30 min
