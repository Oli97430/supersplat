Place a 256×256 .ico file at: assets/icon.ico
(referenced by setup.iss as SetupIconFile and for shortcuts)

Quick way to create one from a PNG:
  - convert via https://convertio.co/png-ico/
  - or use ImageMagick: magick icon.png -define icon:auto-resize=256,128,64,48,32,16 icon.ico

Optional wizard images (164×314 and 55×58 BMP):
  - assets/wizard-large.bmp  (164×314)
  - assets/wizard-small.bmp  (55×58)

If you don't provide these, comment out the related lines in setup.iss.
